--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT IT IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Story 4.5: "A single command turns a recorded flight into a result with a
verdict for each inspection point." `dronedeck process --flight <id>` loads
the flight's readings and the plan it flew, runs clean → classify → interpret
ONCE over the whole flight, and writes
<data folder>/results/<flight id>/result.json. The owner's ask (2026-10-09):
the dirty data against the clean, and what it means — by BLOCKS of change,
not every wiggle, with the readings as the evidence.

3. DECISIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• ON THE LAPTOP, IN THE AGENT — not Supabase. Edge Functions are Deno only with
  2 s of CPU a request; the laptop has every reading the moment the drone
  lands, and needs no network to judge them. PORTS AND ADAPTERS (FlightSource, ResultSink),
  stages chosen in one composition root: a cloud worker later is two adapters.
• THE UNIT OF WORK IS THE FLIGHT (contract v2, 2026-10-09 — CHANGED from one
  inspection point). A block of unusual readings can start in transit and end
  at a point; a stage that sees one point at a time cannot find it, and v1
  never analysed transit at all. Readings carry their point_id; interpret
  gives every point its verdict from them.
• AFTER THE DATA HAS ARRIVED, NEVER IN FLIGHT (2026-11-15, contract v3). A
  flight is processed once it has landed and its CSV is closed. Story 4.9's
  live verdicts — the flight so far judged as each point's hold ended — were
  removed: the owner's ask is offline processing of what has arrived.
• NO CAMERA, NO FRAMES (2026-11-15, contract v3). v2 handed every stage the
  session's frames, enhanced them (clahe@1), measured the view change
  (scene@1) and attached frames to each finding. The AI deck is off the
  drone, so all of that went; a finding rests on the readings alone. Photos
  are taken by hand and classified on their own (story 4.4).
• A STAGE THAT FAILS OR BREAKS THE CONTRACT never takes the flight with it:
  clean failing leaves every point insufficient_data (unchecked readings are
  not analysed); classify failing labels the sensors unknown; interpret
  failing leaves every point insufficient_data; each failure recorded with
  its reason.
• NO INVENTED THRESHOLD. Every number in a stage cites a measurement file:
  ml/sensor-faults/MEASUREMENTS.txt, ml/anomaly-eval/MEASUREMENTS.txt. Severities are tied to the measured wander
  of normal flights and stay PROVISIONAL until a real heat source is measured.
• THE TELEMETRY IS THE SOURCE OF TRUTH (the owner). A photo's verdict never
  makes or changes a finding.
• RUNTIME DEPENDENCIES: numpy (clean, classify). opencv-python-headless is no
  longer used by a stage; it stays for the photo classifier (story 4.4).
  scipy and ruptures are NOT shipped (ruptures is a test dependency, the
  reference PELT).
• THE DPP SWITCH (2026-09-29; moved and defaulted 2026-10-09): at the TOP of
  the Control page in Auto and Manual alike, ON BY DEFAULT IN BOTH (Manual was
  off until the owner asked). Settable before a session starts or during one;
  the choice lasts until the session ends; audited; read as a flight BEGINS.
• `dronedeck process --all` processes every recorded flight with no result
  or one from an older pipeline, and queues each for the web; a flight with no
  readings is skipped. Run again, it does only what is left.
• A CHILD PROCESS, one flight at a time, lower priority, 15 minutes at most —
  never in the agent's process (the interpreter lock beside the 50 Hz loop; a
  hung stage must never take the radio with it).
• PROCESSING RESUMES WHEN THE APP STARTS AGAIN (2026-10-09, the owner's ask):
  flights left pending, queued or running are queued again, in the
  background; a FAILED one is not retried unasked. Each laptop processes its
  OWN flights — it holds every reading. DRONEDECK_RESUME=0 turns
  it off; the tests turn it off (tests/conftest.py).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. INVARIANTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Nothing is processed while the drone flies: processing.py has two kinds of
  job, "flight" and "session", both queued after the data has arrived.
• CLOSING THE QUEUE STARTS NOTHING THAT WAS WAITING (ProcessingQueue.close,
  the agent's shutdown). A job started then outlives the agent and its result
  is never recorded, so the next start runs it again. Decided by `_closed`,
  not by where the stop marker sits: on feat/no-camera the marker became a
  FIFO entry and every waiting job — session.end()'s own included — ran after
  close() (found and fixed 2026-11-16; test_processing.py
  test_closing_starts_nothing_that_was_still_waiting, and the child started
  during close() is stopped too).
• The pipeline imports nothing from flight, session, api, sync or camera —
  tests/pipeline/test_conformance.py rule 1, per implementation. (safety is
  allowed: the cleaner imports the flight guard's speed limit, one number.)
• Every implementation in tests/pipeline/conformance.py passes the contract's
  checked rules on the whole flight, on each point, and on an empty point.
• Temperatures arrive in the flight's own unit; an absolute value takes
  × 9/5 + 32, a difference × 9/5 only — °F and °C give the same flags, the
  same events, the same severities (tested in each stage).
• Re-running a flight replaces its result.json (atomic) and, once uploaded,
  its row and findings on the web; a finding keeps its id.
• The resume never runs on import of api/rest.py (built at import): a test
  must not start processing the laptop's real flights.
