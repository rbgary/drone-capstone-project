--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT IT IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The BMP388 sits on the main board beside the STM32 and the motor drivers. Its
temperature is the room PLUS heat the drone makes. ThermalEngine is Matthew's
engine from the previous team (flight_sensor_test_with_recovery_and_geofencing.py
and lawnmower_flight.py, class ThermalEngine), ported with its constants
unchanged: one offset measured at startup, scaled by a three-step thrust table
(idle 1.00, under 35000 thrust 0.62, above it 0.78), re-estimated only with
the motors off. Validated by that team at 0.12 °F mean absolute error against
a 72 °F reference. Its constants are not changed without new data.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CPU AND THRUST HEAT BIASES (SPRINT 3, TASK 7 — Yordanos) — STARTER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: "reflected temperature values remain stable over time if the ambient
room conditions do not change".

Two biases the step table cannot follow (heat_bias.py):

  CPU WARM-UP   the board keeps warming for minutes after power-on, so the
                startup offset is too small later and the reading creeps up.
                cpu(t) = A · (1 − e^(−t/τ))
  THRUST HEAT   motor and driver heat arrives and leaves slowly; the table
                switches instantly. thrust fraction through a first-order lag
                with gain G and time constant τ_thrust.

The engine subtracts how much each has GROWN since it started. It is OPT-IN:
ThermalEngine(..., heat_bias=HeatBiasModel(HeatBiasTuning(...))). With no
model, or the default all-zero tuning, the output is exactly as before.
Only `dronedeck thermal-test` passes one (BENCH_CPU_WARMUP, section 3); a
flight's engine (reader.py) still runs without. (This said "nothing in the
agent passes a model yet" until 2026-11-26: drift from the 2026-11-07 fit.)

Measured in simulation only (a 22.00 °C room, a board warming 1.5 °C with
τ = 240 s): after 15 minutes the plain engine reads 22.55 °C, with the model
22.00 °C. Real coefficients have to come from the drone.

FITTING. Record a motors-off bench run (task 8 below makes one), then:
    python -m dronedeck.telemetry.heat_bias fit recording.csv
prints A, τ and the fit error. The thrust terms need a recording with the
motors running at known thrust — not fitted yet.

FIRST FIT (2026-11-07): bench run thermal-bench-20261107-155544.csv, 5 min,
room 71.3 °F. Plain engine: 72.0 °F -> 73.1 °F (crept +1.1 °F), mean error
1.0 °F. Fit: A = 6.14 °C, τ = 130 s, rms 0.09 °C → heat_bias.py
BENCH_CPU_WARMUP. Replayed with it: 71.3 -> 71.4 °F, mean error 0.05 °F —
fitted and checked on the SAME run; a second run must confirm it.
`dronedeck thermal-test` uses it by default (--no-warmup: without).

3. THE BENCH TEST — SENSORS ONLY (SPRINT 3, TASK 8 — Yordanos) — STARTER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: "run the ThermalEngine test outside of flight missions, with the motors
stationary".

Matthew's flight_sensor_test_with_recovery_and_geofencing.py ran SensorLogger
during a two-layer serpentine flight. telemetry/bench.py is the same engine
with the flight removed; the CLI opens the link read-only (as `stations` does),
never arms and sends no setpoint:

    dronedeck thermal-test --minutes 10 --ambient 74F --reference 74.2F

It samples at 2 Hz (Matthew's LOG_PERIOD_MS = 500), writes
flights/thermal-bench/thermal-bench-<time>.csv in the data folder, prints a
line every 10 s, and ends with a summary: corrected temperature at the start
and end (one-minute means), the drift between them, and the mean absolute
error against --reference. A sample with any motor turning is kept in the CSV
(motors_on=1), left out of the summary, and warned about.

The CSV is what section 2's fit reads.

4. THE TAKEOFF COOLING RAMP — ON BY DEFAULT (2026-11-07 — Yordanos)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Measured: two lab missions on 2026-11-07 (7148a651, fca56de0), room 71.1 °F
on a thermometer. With the step table the corrected reading was 4.5 °F warm
at takeoff, 1.5-1.9 °F at 16-20 s, 0.4 °F at 45 s — the same curve in both
flights. The error follows TIME SINCE TAKEOFF, not hold time: the board takes
tens of seconds to cool to where the 0.78 / 0.62 scale assumes it already is.

Change (correction.py, CorrectionTuning.flight_cooling_tau_s = 20 s): in
flight the scale slides from scale_idle (1.00) to its band's value,
    scale(t) = 1.00 + (target − 1.00) · (1 − e^(−t/τ)),
t from the first sample with thrust; landing (thrust 0) restarts it. The end
values 0.78 / 0.62 are Matthew's, unchanged. 0 restores the instant step.

Fit: both flights replayed through ThermalEngine at τ = 0 (old), 5-40 s.
τ = 20 s: mean in-flight error 2.4 °F -> 0.2 °F; per-point means within
0.4 °F (old: up to 3.3 °F). Short holds (1-5 s) are fine — no warm-up hold
needed.

NOT DONE: confirmed on a new mission (on main since PR #9, 2026-11-22;
built first on branch heat-bias-correction); only two flights, one room.
