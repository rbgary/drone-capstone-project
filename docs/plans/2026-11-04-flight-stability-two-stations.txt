FLIGHT STABILITY WITH TWO BASE STATIONS — the open needs, not yet a plan
================================================================================

THE ASK (Hannah, 2026-11-04): stabilize the drone's flight and allow smooth
take-off, directional changes and landing with two base stations. Hard to test
without the hardware, so the first step was to find what the code and docs
already record as needed. This is that survey. Nothing in it is built yet.
Each item names where it is recorded, so it can be re-checked against the code
before work starts.

TWO FACTS THAT SHAPE ALL OF IT

1. TAKE-OFF, MOVING AND LANDING ALL GO THROUGH THE MANUAL FLIGHT SYSTEM —
   flight/manual.py, flight/keyframe.py, flight/tuning.py. Missions fly
   through it too: the mission controller gives it goals (hold_at, fly_to,
   land) and never flies the drone itself (missions/mission-controller.txt).
   Those files were locked to Samuel until 2026-11-04, when the team removed
   the lock (features/backend/manual-control.txt): anyone may change them now.
   Items marked MANUAL FILES below are in them — re-fly the ladder in
   flight-log.txt after changing any of them.
2. SEVERAL CAUSES ARE HARDWARE. On 2026-10-24, with two stations up, the
   positioning deck saw no light on any of its four sensors
   (flight-log.txt, "TWO STATIONS UP, AND THE DECK SEES NO LIGHT"), and no
   flight with base-station positioning has run on the current drone.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STABILITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- THRUST HEADROOM. The old drone needed ~81 % of full thrust to hover
  (~53,000 of 65,535), the AI deck on top as the payload — little left to
  level or turn with. Not measured on the current drone.
  [features/backend/flight-status.txt, UPDATES 6 and 7]  HARDWARE: fly without
  the AI deck, or fit the thrust upgrade.
- THE TUNING IS PROVISIONAL. tuning.py's height-loop gains are "starting
  values from two flights … not a finished tune", measured on the OLD drone,
  barometer only. UPDATE 7: re-verify before any mission.
  [features/backend/flight-tuning.txt]  MANUAL FILES; needs flight traces.
- POSITION ASSISTANCE IS WHAT MADE IT SMOOTH. The flights held to 1.3–1.8 cm
  were assisted (base stations with geometry); nearly every rough one was
  barometer-only. Getting the stations working is the largest stability gain,
  and it is not code. [flight-status.txt, UPDATE 5]
- THE MISSION CONSTANTS ARE GUESSES. ARRIVE_M 0.10, SETTLE_S 1.0,
  SETTLE_TIMEOUT_S 10.0, TRANSIT_MARGIN_S 5.0 — "each a guess until the lab
  replaces it with a measurement".
  [backend/agent/dronedeck/mission/controller/mission_controller.py,
   handoffs/sprint-1/done/mission-verification.txt]  Not locked; needs lab
  flights, then `dronedeck mission-report` suggests values.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TAKE-OFF
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- FIXED, NEVER FLOWN: the controller rebuilt before every flight (so a crash's
  wound-up integrators do not carry into the next take-off), beam blackouts
  ridden out to 3 s on the filter's own variance, telemetry gaps to 2.0 s.
  [flight-status.txt, UPDATE 5, "WHAT IS FIXED BUT HAS NOT FLOWN"]  Lab check,
  not new code.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LANDING — the clearest code-level need
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- THE DRONE LOCKS ITSELF AFTER SOME LANDINGS. Flight 0fca5fb4 (2026-10-05):
  the manual system declared "landed" at z 0.055 m with thrust ~47,600 and
  motor m1 ~59,900 — still 5.5 cm up, motors high — then cut them; within six
  minutes supervisor.info read LOCKED, cleared only by a restart. Three
  re-flights then ran armed → flying → landing with thrust 0. Whether the cut
  from 5 cm is what trips the lock is NOT YET MEASURED. Today the mission path
  refuses and offers Reset drone.
  [missions/mission-controller.txt, "FOUND, NOT FIXED";
   missions/how-autonomous-flight-works.txt, "KNOWN, NOT YET SOLVED"]  MANUAL FILES
  (the landing is manual.py's).
- A TOTAL RADIO LOSS STILL DROPS THE DRONE: the firmware cuts the motors at
  about 2 s and does not glide. Only a lost downlink can be landed gently.
  [flight-status.txt, UPDATE 5]  Firmware behaviour.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DIRECTIONAL CHANGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- THE LEG TIMEOUT (T8) treats a 3-D leg as flown at the mission's speed, but
  the height moves at CLIMB_RATE_M_S (0.15 m/s): a 0.7 m climb at Brisk
  (0.20 m/s) takes ~4.7 s against 3.5 s + TRANSIT_MARGIN_S — only the margin
  absorbs it. The mission report already plans legs the planner's way, so the
  report and T8 disagree. [missions/mission-controller.txt, "FOUND, NOT
  FIXED"]  OUTSIDE THE MANUAL FILES; testable in the simulator (tests/sim_drone.py,
  tests/mission/test_controller.py).
- THE EARLIER ARROW COMPLAINTS ("the wrong way", "drifts sideways") were traced
  to having no position, not to the key mapping. [flight-status.txt, UPDATE 6]

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TWO BASE STATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- NEVER FLOWN WITH TWO. ② Position measures every station from one press now
  (it used to keep the first and erase the second) — not yet run on the drone.
  [flight-log.txt 2026-10-24; features/desktop/auto-control.txt]
- ONE STATION IS THE DEFAULT. A room with two does not insist on both unless
  DRONEDECK_MIN_STATIONS=2 is set; flight_guard.py calls requiring both "worth
  doing wherever they exist" — only two give an occluded deck somewhere else
  to look. [backend/agent/dronedeck/safety/flight_guard.py, required_stations]
  OUTSIDE THE MANUAL FILES; small and testable. (Since 2026-11-04 an extra unmeasured
  station no longer blocks ② Position — features/backend/flight-safety.txt.)
- A MOVED STATION IS NOT DETECTED. Base-station geometry is not re-checked at
  Start: if a station moved since the room was drawn, absolute positions mean
  something else. The fix — the room's geometry fingerprint — is designed and
  NOT BUILT. [missions/floor-plans.txt; missions/mission-controller.txt,
  DECISIONS]  OUTSIDE THE MANUAL FILES; testable without the drone.
